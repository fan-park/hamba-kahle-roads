Route guide

Dundee to Nqutu

Route R68 eastAbout 50 kmDrive 45 minutes to 1 hour

A short rural run on the R68 through battlefields country.

When to drive

  • Drive in daylight if you can: livestock, pedestrians and rough sections, with few streetlights.
  • Taxis stop often, especially on weekday mornings, late afternoons and at month-end.
  • December: busier around Christmas and New Year as people travel home.

Typical patterns, not guarantees. Check official sources before you go.

Before you leave

  • Fill up in Dundee; there's little in between.
  • Drive in daylight if you can: livestock and pedestrians are common and there are few streetlights.
  • Minibus taxis stop often, so keep a safe following distance.

Dundee to Nqutu

Leaving DundeeLane adviceRoad know-how

The R68 and R33 share the road for the first few kilometres east of Dundee. Stay on the R68 where the R33 splits off towards Vryheid.

Livestock and pedestriansHazardRoad know-how

Cattle, goats and people on or beside the road, especially near villages and at dusk.

Rough sectionsHazardRoad know-how

Potholes and broken edges in places. Slow down and don't swerve suddenly.

Taxis stoppingSafetyRoad know-how

Minibus taxis pull over to drop off and pick up, sometimes without warning. Keep a gap and don't overtake on blind rises.

BattlefieldsTipRoad know-how

Isandlwana and Rorke's Drift are signposted in this area if you're visiting the battlefields.

Spotted something on the road?
