Langebaan (West Coast) to Cape Town

Route R27 West Coast Road via Table View and YzerfonteinAbout 125 kmDrive About 1½ hours

A quick, scenic run up the R27 with long straights, strong wind and the West Coast National Park on the way.

This guide was written for Cape Town to Langebaan (West Coast). The same roads apply on your trip, but read exits, merges and lane tips the other way round.

When to drive

  • Avoid the R27 through Table View at weekday peaks, roughly 06:30 to 08:30 and 16:00 to 18:00.
  • The south-easter is strongest on summer afternoons, so mornings are calmer for the crosswind stretches.
  • Saturday mornings heading out and Sunday afternoons heading back are busy, and flower season (August to September) brings extra traffic to the West Coast National Park.
  • December and Easter: busiest at the start and end of the holidays.

Typical patterns, not guarantees. Check official sources before you go.

Before you leave

  • No toll plazas.
  • Fill up in Table View or Melkbosstrand; there's little until Langebaan.
  • Flower season (around August and September) brings heavy weekend traffic into the park.
  • Check West Coast National Park gate times if you're driving through it.

Langebaan to Melkbosstrand

Tortoises and buckSafetyRoad know-how

Wildlife crosses the road, especially in the national park. Stick to the park's speed limits.

Long straightsHazardRoad know-how

Easy to speed and to doze. Keep an eye on your speed and take a break if needed.

Strong crosswindsWeatherRoad know-how

The southeaster blows hard across the R27 in summer, with blowing sand. Hold the wheel firmly, especially towing.
