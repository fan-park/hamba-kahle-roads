Hamba Kahle Roads

About

Know the road before you go

Hamba kahle means "go well". Hamba Kahle Roads helps South African drivers plan a trip with the things a satnav doesn't tell you: potholes, risky stretches, which lane to be in, tolls and fuel stops, plus recent reports from other drivers.

Who runs it

Hamba Kahle Roads is run by Superfan, a registered South African company. It's free to use, with no banner ads, and we don't sell personal information.

Where the information comes from

  • Route guides are written by us from public sources and local knowledge, and were last checked in October 2026. They're general guidance, not live information. Roads, tolls and roadworks change.
  • Driver reports come from people using the site. Each shows when it was posted, and other drivers can tap "Still there" or "Gone now". A report fades once more drivers say it's gone than still there, and reports drop off the site after 14 days.
  • Official sources such as SANRAL and N3 Route are linked on the home page. Check them for closures before you leave.

No reports doesn't mean no hazards. It only means nobody has reported anything yet.

How reports are moderated

  • Anyone signed in can flag a report as spam, abusive, fake or containing private information. Flags are private.
  • A flagged report is hidden straight away for the person who flagged it, and from everyone once three different drivers flag it.
  • We check flagged reports and remove ones that break the terms. If you think your report was hidden unfairly, contact us and we'll take another look.
  • Report locations are rounded to about 100 m. Reports show only the display name you choose, never your email address or account.

Stay safe

Never use the site while driving. Let a passenger report, or wait until you've stopped somewhere safe. Always follow road signs and traffic officers, even if the site says something different. In an emergency, call 112 from a cellphone.

Get help or tell us something

Spotted a mistake in a route guide, something not working, or have an idea? Use the Feedback button on the site (no sign-in needed), or email us. If you're not receiving sign-in emails, check your spam folder, then let us know.
